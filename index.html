Your time to reset

Four days to slow down, reconnect, and feel inspired.

Step away from everyday routines and rediscover the pleasure of moving gently, creating with your hands, and sharing meaningful moments. This intimate retreat brings together yoga, mindfulness, pottery, and sound healing in a peaceful Mallorcan setting.

  1. 01

    Mindful Movement

    Daily yoga and moments of conscious breathing.

  2. 02

    Creative Exploration

    A hands-on pottery workshop designed to spark creativity.

  3. 03

    Deep Restoration

    Sound healing, nourishing meals, and time to simply be.

The experience

Moments, gathered slowly.

Morning practice beneath the olives, clay between your fingers, quiet afternoons by the water.

Your home in Mallorca

A peaceful hideaway at Hotel Curolla

Discover the quiet charm of Maria de la Salut, a traditional Mallorcan village away from the busiest tourist routes. Thoughtful interiors, local character, and a welcoming atmosphere create the perfect setting for a few days of rest and reconnection.

  • Boutique hotel in a traditional stone townhouse
  • Quiet village setting away from tourist routes
  • Shared double rooms with separate single beds, or single rooms
  • Garden courtyard and pool for slow afternoons
  • Daily vegan breakfast and dinner
